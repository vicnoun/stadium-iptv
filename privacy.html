Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Version française

Stadium IPTV is a video player for Mac and iPhone. The app does not collect, store on our servers or sell any personal data.

Data kept on your device

Your source addresses, credentials (stored in Apple's Keychain), favorites, folders, playback positions and settings stay on your device. They are deleted with the app, or from its settings.

Network connections

The app only connects to the servers you add yourself (IPTV provider, Jellyfin server) to load channels, the programme guide and videos. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Pro purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive any payment information.

Analytics and advertising

The app uses no analytics, no trackers and no advertising.

Content

Stadium IPTV does not provide any channels or content. You are responsible for the sources you add.
